2.9 | Statistical analysis
All statistical analyses were performed using stepwise general‐
ized linear and logistic mixed models (GLMMs) on the lme4 (Bates,
Maechler, Bolker, & Walker, 2015) and nlme (Pinheiro, Bates,
DebRoy, & Sarkar, 2017) packages in R (R Core Team, 2013). Model
selection was based on Akaike’s information criterion (AIC) scores,
where the decision to include a new fixed‐effect variable or accept
one iteration of a model over another required a reduction in AIC
of at least 2 (Burnham & Anderson, 2002). The simpler model was
always chosen in the case of equal models (Burnham & Anderson,
2002). Additionally, linear models were compared using maximum
likelihood tests. For logistic models, odds ratios were calculated
using exponentiated estimates. In order to control for the potential
